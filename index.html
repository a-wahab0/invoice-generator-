Terms & Privacy

Where your invoices are stored, by default: this app saves everything — your business details, clients, line items, logo, and every invoice you build — in your browser's local storage on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to a server, and no account is required to use the app.

What that means in practice: your invoices are only available on this browser, on this device. Clearing your browser's site data, using a different browser, or switching devices means you won't see them there. We can't recover locally-stored data for you if it's cleared — there's no server-side copy to restore from.

Optional account (sync): if you choose to sign up, your invoices are instead saved to your own private Firebase account so you can reach them from other devices. This is entirely opt-in — nobody is required to sign up to use Invoice Gen. If you sign up, your invoice data is transmitted to and stored by Firebase (Google Cloud) under your account, protected by access rules that restrict it to you.

PDFs are generated entirely on your device — the PDF file never passes through any server either way.

No ads, no tracking, no data resale. This tool doesn't sell or share your data with third parties.
